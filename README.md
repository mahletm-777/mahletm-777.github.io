I currently work as an LLM and agentic AI evaluator at Scale AI, where I design and run evaluations that probe model reasoning, tool use, and reliability.

I recently completed my PhD in Materials Science and Engineering, where I used Python for machine learning and data science to understand and predict material behavior from experimental and simulation data.

I am transitioning into technical AI safety. I completed the BlueDot Impact Technical AI Safety course and an Apart Research sprint on AI collusion.
